# Product and Technical Glossary

## MPA

Ethiopian Muslim Professionals Association.

## Member

A person with an active or otherwise eligible MPA membership status. Having an account alone does not make a person a member.

## Professional Profile

The structured professional identity of a person. It can contain education, experience, skills, services, certifications and professional interests.

## Vetted Professional

A professional whose relevant claims have passed the applicable MPA verification workflow. Vetted does not mean every piece of information is verified.

## Verification Claim

A specific assertion being reviewed, such as profession, education or certification.

## Evidence

A document, image, record or other information used to support a verification or project decision.

## Organization

An institution or group acting as a stakeholder, employer, partner, project participant or event organizer.

## Exclusive Job

A job whose eligibility rules restrict application to a configured member/verification/qualification audience.

## Service Listing

A professional offering describing work a member can provide.

## Service Request

A stakeholder request to engage a listed professional service.

## Mentor

A professional approved to provide structured mentoring.

## Mentee

A person participating in a mentoring program as the recipient of mentoring.

## Mentorship

The structured relationship/program between mentor and mentee.

## Event

A scheduled activity managed through the platform.

## Rescheduling

A controlled change to event date/time/venue that preserves registration relationships and notifies impacted users.

## Learning Record

Normalized MPA record representing enrollment, progress/completion or certification information originating from a learning provider.

## Moodle

External learning management system integrated with MPA.

## Project

A structured community or institutional activity with objectives, participants, resources, progress and evidence.

## Field Evidence

Evidence captured during project field activity, potentially including photos, location and findings.

## Inspection

A formal operational review of project implementation/evidence.

## Donation

A financial contribution linked to a project or approved cause.

## Payment

A financial transaction/attempt used to settle a business obligation such as membership, event fee or donation.

## Provider

An external system used for a specific capability, such as Telebirr, Moodle, SMS, email or calendar.

## Domain Event

An internal record indicating that a meaningful business event occurred, such as membership activation or event rescheduling.

## Idempotency

A property that makes repeated processing of the same command/event safe and prevents duplicate side effects.

## Scope

The boundary within which a permission applies, such as self, organization, project, geography or global.

## Audit Log

An append-oriented record of significant business/security actions.

## Correction

A controlled request for an applicant/user to fix specific information or evidence before a workflow can proceed.

## Active Membership

A membership period that currently grants configured benefits.

## Grace Period

A configured period after expiry during which selected benefits may continue under explicit policy.

## Stakeholder

An organization or individual interacting with MPA for recruitment, services, events, projects, learning or partnership.

## Trusted Professional Ecosystem

The product concept combining membership, verified capability, opportunities, development and community participation into a connected platform.
